Frequently Asked Questions

01What is Microsoft Power Platform and how can it help my business?+
A suite of low-code tools — Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, and Copilot — that lets businesses build custom apps, automate workflows, analyze data, and use AI to improve productivity and decisions.
02Why should I choose Powercraft for Power Platform consulting?+
Deep expertise in Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, and Copilot, with a focus on governance, scalability, and business transformation across startups to enterprises.
03Can Power Platform integrate with SharePoint, SAP, or Dynamics 365?+
Yes — Power Platform integrates with Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Dynamics 365, Azure, SAP, and many third-party systems via connectors, APIs, and custom integrations.
04How long does it take to build and launch a Power Apps solution?+
As little as 2–6 weeks depending on complexity, with enterprise-grade scalability and governance built in.
05Do you offer training and support for Power Platform users?+
Yes — tailored training, ongoing support, and enablement services to build confident citizen developers.
